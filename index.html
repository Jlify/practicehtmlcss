The Mercedes-Benz Group AG (former Daimler AG) is one of the world's most successful automotive companies. With Mercedes-Benz AG, the Group offers a wide range of high-end passenger cars and premium vans. Mercedes-Benz Financial Services is another key pillar of the Group and plays a central role in the financial services business.

Classic Mercedes-Benz models are globally renowned for their unmatched mechanical over-engineering, extreme durability, and timeless aesthetic, built in an era when uncompromising material quality and repairability were the ultimate priorities.

Modern Mercedes-Benz models represent the pinnacle of automotive digitization, cutting-edge luxury, and advanced connectivity, shifting the brand's focus from pure mechanical longevity toward software-driven innovation and complex assistance systems.

Mercedes-Benz customer service balances the specialized preservation of historical vehicles through dedicated heritage networks with the high-tech, proactive care required for modern digital luxury platforms.
